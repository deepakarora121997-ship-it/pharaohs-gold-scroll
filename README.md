# Royal Egyptian Union

Create a stunning, interactive, cinematic Egyptian-style luxury wedding invitation website for Waleed & Irinka.

Wedding details:
Waleed & Irinka
Wedding Ceremony
12 September 2026
5:00 PM

The website should have a royal ancient Egyptian theme, inspired by Egyptian palaces, pyramids, temples, pharaoh architecture, hieroglyphics, lotus flowers, scarabs, golden ornaments, and luxury royal decorations.

Start with a dramatic full-screen animation of massive ancient Egyptian golden temple doors slowly opening. Behind the doors, reveal a breathtaking cinematic view of pyramids at sunset with floating golden particles and magical lighting.

Display:

WALEED & IRINKA
Request the honour of your presence at their Wedding Ceremony

Add an elegant animated button: OPEN INVITATION

After clicking, smoothly transition into a luxurious scrolling wedding invitation experience.

Use a premium color palette of metallic gold, deep royal blue, black, ivory, and warm desert sand.

Include these sections:

Welcome Section – Waleed & Irinka with elegant Egyptian royal decorations.

Save The Date – Display 12 • 09 • 2026 in large luxurious typography.

Wedding Ceremony – Display 12 September 2026 | 5:00 PM.

Countdown Timer – Countdown to the wedding day.

Love Story Section – Beautiful romantic image placeholders framed with Egyptian golden ornaments.

RSVP Section – Elegant button saying RSVP NOW with a guest confirmation form.

Final Section – A breathtaking night view of Egyptian pyramids under the stars with glowing golden lights.

Display the final message:

WALEED & IRINKA
Together with their families invite you to celebrate the beginning of their forever.

12 September 2026 • 5:00 PM

Make the website highly interactive, mobile-first, responsive, cinematic, smooth, elegant and luxurious. Include smooth scrolling, fade transitions, parallax effects, subtle floating particles, glowing golden elements, animated hieroglyphics, and premium typography.

Do not make it look like a normal website template. Make it feel like an immersive, high-end Egyptian royal wedding invitation experience.
